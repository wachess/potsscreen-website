POTS Screen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

POTS Screen ("the app") helps you record and review POTS-related heart-rate data. This policy explains exactly what the app stores and how it is handled.

WHAT THE APP COLLECTS

  • Heart-rate readings measured by your watch's sensor.
  • Step counts / activity measured by your watch.
  • Heart-rate "alert" events (a heart-rate rise while you are at rest).
  • Standing-test results, including the heart-rate samples recorded during each test.
  • Symptoms, ratings and notes you choose to add to a standing test in the phone app (kept on your phone only).
  • Your date of birth, which you enter once during setup.

HOW THE APP USES THIS DATA

  • To display your charts, history, and trends.
  • To run the guided standing test and compare your heart-rate rise against commonly used screening thresholds (your date of birth selects the teen or adult threshold).
  • To notify you when your resting heart rate rises without matching activity.

WHERE YOUR DATA IS STORED

  • Everything above is stored only on your watch and the paired phone, inside each app's private storage.
  • Data syncs directly between your watch and your phone over the Wear OS (Google Wearable Data Layer) connection. None of it is sent to the developer, and none of it is sent to any third party.
  • The app has no account or login, and shows no advertising.
  • Automatic cloud backup is disabled, so this data is not copied to Google's backup servers.

USAGE AND CRASH REPORTS

  • To help improve the app, both apps send basic usage information (such as which features are used) and crash reports to Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics). These include standard device details such as device model, Android version and approximate region, and are tied to a random identifier for each installation — not to your name or any account.
  • No health, medical or other sensitive information is ever sent.
  • Google handles this information as described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

YOUR CONTROLS

  • Retention: choose how long passive tracking data is kept (Storage screen). Standing tests are kept until you delete them.
  • Delete: clear heart-rate, step, or alert data — or delete data before a chosen date — from the Storage screen.
  • Export: save a copy of your data (CSV files plus standing tests) to back up or share wherever you choose.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its stored data from that device, and stops any further usage or crash reports from it.

CHILDREN

This app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children.

MEDICAL DISCLAIMER

POTS Screen is a screening aid, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about your results.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with the app.
