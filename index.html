POTS Screen

A private, on-device companion for tracking POTS-related heart-rate symptoms — no accounts, no cloud, no tracking.

Android + Wear OS

What it does

Guided Standing Test

Run a guided orthostatic screening test that compares your heart-rate rise against commonly used clinical thresholds, adjusted for teens vs. adults.

Passive Heart-Rate Monitoring

Your watch continuously tracks heart rate and step counts, and flags unusual resting heart-rate spikes automatically.

Charts & History

Review your heart-rate trends, standing test results, and activity history at a glance.

Full Data Control

Choose how long data is kept, export it as CSV anytime, or delete it — all from the Storage screen.

Runs as a Wear OS watch app paired with an Android phone companion app. All heart-rate, step, and standing-test data stays on your devices — nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.
